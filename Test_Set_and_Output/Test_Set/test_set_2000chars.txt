The system employs the Least Significant Bit (LSB) technique for image steganography and an append-based method for audio and video encoding. The application is developed using Java Spring Boot, MySQL, and a responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript frontend with a modern dark space-themed user interface. 1. IntroductionIn today's digital world, the security and privacy of communication have become critical concerns. Two primary techniques exist for securing digital communications: cryptography and steganography. Cryptography transforms the message into an unreadable format, making the existence of the message obvious but its content hidden. Steganography, on the other hand, conceals the very existence of the message by embedding it within a carrier medium such as an image, audio file, or video. The word steganography comes from the Greek words steganos meaning hidden, and graphia meaning writing. Historically, techniques include invisible ink and microdots. In the digital domain, it involves hiding data within files without causing perceptible changes.2. Literature Review2.1 Digital Image Steganography: Several researchers have explored image techniques. The LSB method, first formalized by Chandramouli and Memon (2001), remains the most popular due to its simplicity and low visual impact. Studies by Petitcolas (1999) evaluated the robustness of these techniques against detection attacks. More recent work by Fridrich (2010) introduced statistical analysis methods for detecting content.2.2 Audio Steganography: Audio has been studied less extensively.
